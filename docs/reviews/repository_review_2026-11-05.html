R-02 · Fano sampler is not Bernoulli for all sub-unity means, contrary to its docstring and the formula note

minordocumentation gapRealisation, transport and sampling

Location. src/event_analysis/production_readout.jl:145-186; docs/production_formulas.tex:59

Evidence.

The docstring says "for means below one, the integer draw is Bernoulli(mean)" and the note states N_i ~ Bernoulli(μ_i) for 0≤μ_i<1. The implementation always calls sample_integer_moments, which uses the adjacent-integer law only when F·μ ≤ f(1−f) (line 153), i.e. for μ ≤ 1−F = 0.85; for 0.85<μ<1 it mixes with Binomial(2, μ/2) (lines 154-159), so such a hit can yield two pairs (verified in source).

Consequence.

Mean and variance targets are met (variance F·μ rather than μ(1−μ)); numerically negligible for 1 mm Geant4 steps (hits of 18.6–21.9 eV). Contract mismatch only.

Proposed remedy.

Either special-case mean<1 → Bernoulli as documented, or correct the docstring and production_formulas.tex.

Validation.

Add a test at μ=0.9 asserting the intended support.

Uncertainty.

None on the code; the policy choice is the user's.

1. Gas

Xenon enriched in Xe-136 with helium. Densities follow the model of the Geant4 geometry: xenon as a real gas with a second-virial compressibility, helium ideal, additive partial volumes.

Potentials along the axis, anode at top

The pads and the SiPM plane are at ground. The potential rises from the TPC-facing face of the IFG through the drift region, the transfer gap and the EL region, and is graded back to ground along the light guides. Only the independent potentials are card values; the ladder is derived.

8. References

  • Gas density: NEXT DocDB 1183 (xenon compressibility), implemented in simulation/nexus/source/geometries/ItacaChamber.cc; enriched xenon isotopic fractions of NEXUS GXeEnriched.
  • Electron drift velocity and diffusion: Magboltz 11.21, run of 2026-11-15 (magboltz/, docs/handoffs/magboltz_xe_he.md).
  • Ion mobility: P. N. B. Neves et al., J. Chem. Phys. 133 (2010) 124316, as plotted in A. F. V. Cortez et al., JINST 12 (2017) P06012, Fig. 3; helium correction by Blanc's law.
  • EL yield: A. F. M. Fernandes et al., JHEP 04 (2020) 034, Table 1 (15 % He). EL variance ratio J: user reading of Oliveira et al., Fig. 6 (2026-11-04).
  • Potentials and grading field: manuscripts/itaca3/itaca3.tex (IFG at 8 kV, sensors at ground) and docs/elm.tex (regions and grading equation).
  • Ion thermal spread: ITACA revisited, arXiv:2604.02443, Eq. (2.1). Qββ: M. Redshaw et al., PRL 98 (2007) 053003.
  • Design values without a literature source are user design inputs of 2026-11-15.

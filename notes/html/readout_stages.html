Detector, electronics and reconstruction

Detector response

  • Ions: drift, diffusion and collection into pad/time buckets.
  • Electrons: attachment, diffusion, EL production and optical collection into SiPM/time buckets.
  • Photons reach the SiPM before PDE sampling. The current ELM delivers blue light after TPB.

Detector interfaces

Electronics response

  • Ions: induced current, linear or nonlinear CSA voltage, correlated noise and ADC counts.
  • Electrons: PDE sampling, SiPM occupancy and recovery, dark activity, ASIC noise and current limit.

Electronics interfaces

Signal reconstruction

  • Reconstruct signed ion charge from ADC counts using the calibrated linear or nonlinear inverse.
  • Correct SiPM response to obtain PE vectors.
  • Calculate event energies before the hit cuts; include mean attachment correction for electrons.
  • Rebin electrons and form itrack and etrack hits.

Signal reconstruction · Event energy and hit output

The production design passes intermediate vectors in memory and writes event totals and retained hits. The implementation plan lists completed and pending work.

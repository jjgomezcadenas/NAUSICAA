From one batch of Monte Carlo events to its hit file: fast trigger, event energy, ion transport, VH1 readout and hits.

1. Scope

  1. The driver scripts/iitaca/run_iitaca_batch.jl reads one batch of Geant4 events and writes one hit file.
  2. The ions are simulated with the modules of the ITACA chain, unchanged: fast trigger and frame (MC fast trigger), ion detector response (detector response), VH1 electronics and reconstruction (VH1 readout), hits (hit output).
  3. The electrons are not simulated. The start time is the Monte Carlo one, and the event energy is the energy deposited in the simulated box, smeared with the resolution of the energy card.
  4. The driver receives IItaca.simulation_inputs of the resolved detector and nothing else of it: no value under a [documentation] table reaches the simulation (cards).

Generation of the Monte Carlo samples

The events are generated with NEXUS (Geant4) in the geometry ItacaChamber: a cylinder of gas, z = 0 at the pad plane and z = length at the EL entry, with no vessel or shielding around it. The macros of each variant are written from the detector cards by scripts/iitaca/write_iitaca_macros.jl into simulation/nexus/macros/iitaca/<variant>/, so the simulated gas and dimensions are those of the cards: gas, pressure, temperature, radius and length from the resolved detector, the rest from the generation card.

  • Inner surface (INNER_SURFACE): a point uniform on the inner surface of the gas cylinder, barrel and both end faces weighted by area, one micrometre inside the gas. It stands for the gammas emitted by the materials around the gas.
  • The gamma samples hold one gamma of the line per event, not the full decay: the other gammas of the decay, such as the 583 keV gamma in coincidence with the ²⁰⁸Tl line, are left out.
  • An event is saved when the energy deposited in the gas lies in the window of the generation card (2300 to 2470 keV). The number generated per batch is stated in the run card, for the efficiencies per generated event.
  • One file per batch; event numbers start at batch × events per batch, and each batch has its own seed.

2. Run card

A run card (config/iitaca/runs/, schema nausicaa.iitaca_run.v1) names the detector set, optionally a variant, and holds the run parameters. Detector values change only under [overrides], applied after those of the variant.

Main track

  • primary_and_delta_electrons: the generated electrons and all their electron descendants whose mother chain contains no gamma. For bb0nu and single electrons.
  • leading_gamma_electron: for events generated as one gamma (²¹⁴Bi, ²⁰⁸Tl). The main track is the electron of highest initial kinetic energy among those produced directly by the generated gamma, with its electron descendants; its creation point is the event position. The other products of the gamma (further Compton electrons, the K X-ray) are gamma deposits: inside the frame they add energy and appear as detached deposits, outside it they belong to the veto.

3. Processing one event

  1. Read the Monte Carlo hits (positions in mm, energies in keV) and the particle table; centre the hits on the event position.
  2. Fast trigger with the fixed frame: a cube of trigger.frame_side_mm around the box of the main track, moved inward at the detector boundary. The event is rejected, in this order, when a main-track hit or an energetic gamma deposit lies outside the detector; when the main track is closer to the EL entry than trigger.el_exclusion_mm; when the track is larger than the frame; when the frame does not fit in the detector; when a gamma deposit above trigger.gamma_energy_cut_keV is not fully inside the frame. The decision of every event is saved.
  3. Box of the ion response: the main track and the gamma deposits inside the frame, enlarged by trigger.simulation_margin_mm and limited to the frame. Only the hits in this box are simulated.
  4. True energy: the sum of the energies of those hits. Event energy: the true energy plus one Gaussian deviate of σ = FWHM(E) / 2.355, with FWHM(E) = energy.resolution_fwhm_at_qbb_keV × √(E / Qββ).
  5. Ions of each hit: mean E / W with the Fano factor.
  6. Ion response: each ion drifts to the pad plane with the thermal spread √(2 k T L / (e E)) for the drift length of the event, in x, y and z; it is assigned to its pad (pitch ions.pad_pitch_mm, random lattice offset per event) and to its frame (ions.frame_ms). All pads overlapping the box are read.
  7. VH1 electronics: converter readings of every pad and frame, with noise.
  8. VH1 reconstruction: ions per pad and frame, and the ion energy of the event (ions × W).
  9. Hits: one per pad and frame with positive reconstructed charge and energy above tracks.saved_hit_cut_keV, at the pad centre and the frame centre, relative to the event position. The cells of the frame outside the simulated box receive noise hits drawn from the Gaussian tail above the cut.

The track selection, the endpoint energies and the ROI cut are applied afterwards, from the hit file.

4. Random streams

Each event has one stream per purpose, seeded with the first 64 bits of SHA-256 of "framed-v1|seed|event number|part" (IItaca.event_seed). An event gives the same result whatever the batch, the order or the other events.

7. Check against the ITACA production

tests/iitaca/test_iitaca_equivalence.jl reads the bb0nu pilot (batches 0 and 1) with the ion settings of the ITACA run bb0nu_itaca_pilot_10x100_ion15 as overrides (config/iitaca/runs/hybrid_equivalence_bb0nu_pilot.toml) and compares with the ITACA hit files.

  • Same accepted events, in the same order: 130 of 164.
  • Same itrack hits, bit for bit: 20,987 hits, positions and energies.
  • Same ion energy and drift length per event; the true energy equals the energy in the frame recorded by the ITACA detector-response stage.

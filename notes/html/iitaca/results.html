Cut flow, ROI last

The network fraction is measured on the selected events in the ROI and applied to all selected events: over this energy range the topology does not depend on the energy. For bb0nu and single electrons it is that of the test sample of the training. The ROI fraction is the fraction of the selected events whose energy is in the ROI.

The ²¹⁴Bi floor

What passes is mostly Compton events whose scattered photon was absorbed within about 1 cm of the main track: two electrons start from almost one point, the topology of a double-beta decay. Compton events are 9.1 % of the selected ²¹⁴Bi at 3 mm and give 184 of the 304 passing events; at 2 mm, 6.4 % and 125 of 199. Photoelectric events behave like single electrons: at 3 mm and 2 mm they pass at 1.73 % and 1.28 %, against 1.34 % and 0.86 % for single electrons; those with no secondary deposit pass at 1.41 % and 1.14 %. Details, mechanism and figures: note on the ²¹⁴Bi Compton floor (PDF).

The 2 mm selection joins segments at 5.3 mm and removes most second electrons beyond 8 mm (124 → 7 events) and many at 4–8 mm (305 → 169). Below 4 mm the acceptance does not improve, 41.4 % at 3 mm and 38.8 % at 2 mm: the second electron touches the main track and the lattice records one object. This class is the floor.

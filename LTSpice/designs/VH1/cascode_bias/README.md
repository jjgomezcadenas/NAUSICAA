# VH1 cascode bias and load length

Generated by `scripts/electronics/design/vh1_cascode_bias.py`; results in
`results/ltspice/design/VH1/cascode_bias/` (`summary.md`, `summary.json`,
noise spectra as CSV). The circuit is the combined variant of the parent
`improvements/` study with the 23 um cascode (M1 110 um, M2 2 um, M3 4 um,
M4 23 um); only the length of the load M3 and the gate bias of the cascode
M4 (`Vncasc`, 0.6621 V in the release) change.

Every variant is re-tuned with the reset loop closed, as in the parent
study: `Vnload` (gate of M3) for the M1 current of 210.3 uA and `Vnmos`
(gate of M2) for an output level of 0.886 V.

| Deck | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ac_<variant>.cir`, `noise_<variant>.cir` | Open-loop gain, input capacitance, charge fraction; output noise spectrum |

Variants: `L3_{4,8}u_casc0.66`, `L3_{4,8,16}u_casc0.76`, `L3_{8,16}u_casc0.86`
(M3 length in um, cascode gate in V).

The output-window scans (`win_*` runs, not kept as decks) lower the level
shift of the closed reset loop step by step, which moves the output down at
a fixed gate voltage as accumulated charge does, and read the open-loop gain
and the charge fraction at each level. They give the lower edge of the
output window at each cascode bias.

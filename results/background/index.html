Both detectors: fiducial gas of radius 2.0 m and length 1.5 m at 16 bar; the copper is 20 cm thick on every face, around the xenon cavity and the passive layers of the shielding cards (config/background/). The pressure vessel (Ti Gr5, about 10 t, 9 Bq of ²¹⁴Bi) is outside the copper and not simulated: behind 20 cm it adds at most 5 % of the ICS ²¹⁴Bi and 1 % of its ²⁰⁸Tl (E₂ attenuation, oblique paths included). The other components of ITACA revisited (fibre barrel, field-cage rings, SiPM boards, EL structure, cathode mesh; for iItaca also the light guides and MARS) are not simulated; in that paper they add 5 % (²¹⁴Bi) and 23 % (²⁰⁸Tl, two thirds from 270 kg of HDPE light guides at 8 µBq/kg) to the ICS.

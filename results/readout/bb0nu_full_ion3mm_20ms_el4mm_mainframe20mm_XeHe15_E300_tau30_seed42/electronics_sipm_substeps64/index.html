64-substep accuracy

  • Reference: individual-microcell simulation, starting fully recovered.
  • Six patterns: dark, weak, steady, clipping edge, isolated pulse, rising and falling signal. 1000 repetitions per pattern.
  • Both calculations use the same sampled optical counts and ASIC noise. Cell assignments and dark avalanches are independent.
  • Failures: charge mean 0; variance 0; clipping probability 0.
  • Largest relative mean difference for buckets with at least 3,000 incident photons: 0.0142%. Largest relative variance difference: 1.744%.
  • Largest clipping-probability difference: 0.60 percentage points, within sampling uncertainty.

Acceptance limits: mean difference ≤ max(0.05 PE, 0.5% of reference mean, 4 standard errors); variance difference ≤ max(0.01 PE², 5% of reference variance, 4 standard errors); clipping-probability difference ≤ max(0.5 percentage points, 4 standard errors).

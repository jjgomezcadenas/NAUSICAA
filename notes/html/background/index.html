ITACA documentation

Radiogenic background

²¹⁴Bi and ²⁰⁸Tl decays in the inner copper shield of iItaca and eITACA: generation in the copper with depth weights, the saved energy window, and the background in the ROI per year and per tonne-year.

Result

²¹⁴Bi and ²⁰⁸Tl from the copper shield in the ROI, iItaca and eITACA: radioactivity budget, window fractions, efficiencies after the window, events per year and per tonne-year, in the format of the ITACA revisited budget.

Model

Each detector is the fiducial gas of its variant (iItaca II_L1500_D350, eITACA IE_L1500_D400: radius 2.0 m, length 1.5 m, 16 bar) inside a xenon cavity closed by the inner copper shield (ICS), 20 cm of copper on the barrel and on both ends. Between the gas and the copper each face has a xenon gap and one passive layer: the fibre barrel (PTFE) on the barrel; on the anode end the EL structure and, for iItaca, the light guides (HDPE); on the cathode end the ion focusing grid and the MARS region (iItaca) or the high-voltage buffer (eITACA). Only the fiducial gas is sensitive. The pressure vessel is outside the copper and not simulated: behind 20 cm it adds at most 5 % of the ICS ²¹⁴Bi and 1 % of its ²⁰⁸Tl (hand calculation with the E₂ attenuation over oblique paths).

Generation in the copper

Full ²¹⁴Bi and ²⁰⁸Tl decays (IonGenerator, region ICS, decay at t = 0; a delayed macro limits the radioactive decay to the generated nucleus), every daughter followed through the copper, the layers and the gas. Nearly all the gammas that reach the gas come from the first few centimetres of copper, so the vertex is drawn as follows and weighted afterwards:

  1. The piece, barrel or one of the two disks, with probability proportional to its volume.
  2. The depth z from the inner face, with density p(z) ∝ e−z/λ truncated at the thickness t (λ = 2.9 cm, the attenuation length of 2.5 MeV gammas in copper; λ = 0 gives a uniform depth).
  3. A uniform position on the surface at that depth: azimuth and axial position (barrel) or azimuth and radius (disks).

The weight of an event, computed from the saved vertex of the primary ion, is w = A(z) / (Vpiece p(z)), with A(z) the area of the surface at depth z (2π (Ri + z) Lcavity for the barrel, π Ro² for a disk) and Vpiece the volume of the piece. Its mean over the sampled vertices is one, so Σw over the events in the window divided by the number of decays generated is the fraction of uniformly distributed decays that reach the window, for any λ. The test draws 4 × 10⁵ vertices with the Julia mirror of the sampler and checks the mean weight, the weighted piece fractions and the weighted depth distribution against the copper volume.

An event is saved when the energy deposited in the fiducial gas lies in 2420–2490 keV, the gamma window of the generation cards: every event that can reach the ROI after the 0.472 % FWHM smearing. The pilot of 10⁶ decays per isotope and detector took 4–7 minutes per task; its window fractions are known to 6–10 %, below the statistical error of the efficiencies after the window, so it is the measurement.

Analysis

  1. Window table (prepare_ics_window.jl): for every saved event the vertex, its piece (checked against the volume name Geant4 recorded), depth and weight; the gas energy from the hits; and the particles that entered the gas, each hit traced to its first ancestor created outside the fiducial gas. Classes: one entering gamma; one entering electron (surface betas); one other; several entering particles, which leave two separate deposits and count as rejected. Output results/background/<name>/pilot/<isotope>/window.h5 (schema nausicaa.background_window.v1): the fraction of the decays in the window per class with its error √Σw² / N, the copper mass, its activity and decays per year.
  2. After the window: the topology of a window event does not depend on where its gamma came from, so the efficiencies measured on the gamma productions of each detector apply: εtrack, the track selection in the ROI per saved window event, and εnet, the network at 80 % signal. iItaca (2 mm pads) from results/iitaca/II_L1000_D500_P2_gamma_window/gamma_window.h5; eITACA from results/eitaca/IE_L1500_D400_results/results.h5, whose ²⁰⁸Tl production was saved on a wider window and is counted on 2420–2490 keV by count_window_events.jl.
  3. Rate (prepare_background_rates.jl): events per year = decays per year × fw(one gamma) × εtrack × εnet; per tonne-year with the xenon mass of the variant. Errors: the window fraction, εtrack and εnet in quadrature; the specific activities (copper 1.2 and 1.4 µBq/kg, ITACA revisited) carry none.

Not simulated: the other components of ITACA revisited (fibre barrel, field-cage rings, SiPM boards, EL structure, cathode mesh; the light guides and MARS of iItaca). In that paper they add 5 % (²¹⁴Bi) and 23 % (²⁰⁸Tl, two thirds from the HDPE light guides at 8 µBq/kg) to the ICS. Each can run as a region in the same geometry when its material and activity are chosen.

iItaca detector and operating conditions

The ion-reading version of ITACA in pure xenon (iItaca, also written ITACAi or II): common design values, the variants II_L1000_D500 and II_L1500_D350, and the quantities derived from them.

Design values: user design inputs, detector set of 2026-11-17. The page is generated from the cards of config/iitaca/ (make iitaca-detector): the values after the overrides of each variant and the quantities derived by src/iitaca/IItaca.jl.

1. Purpose and principle

  1. The ions give the track. The Xe2+ molecular ions (written Xe2+, charge +e) drift to the TopMetal pads with a thermal spread of 1.00 to 1.46 mm at full drift and are read on a cubic 3 mm lattice.
  2. The electrons give the start time, the fast trigger and the event energy. They drift to the EL plane, read by SiPMs at 8 mm pitch.
  3. The gas is pure xenon enriched in Xe-136, without helium. The electron spread at full drift is about 10 mm, so the electron plane gives a coarse image and the track topology comes from the ions.
  4. The simulation covers the ion side: Geant4, ion transport, VH1 electronics, reconstruction, hits and tracks. The electron side is emulated: the fast trigger from the Geant4 deposits and the energy from their sum smeared with the energy resolution.

2. Common design values

Values of the default set. A quantity that a variant changes is marked and listed in Section 3. The simulation reads the gas, the fiducial radius and length, the drift field, the ion velocity, the ion plane (pitch, frame, VH1 card), the energy and the trigger. The other values, kept under [documentation] tables in the cards, give the potential ladder and the light budget of this page: thicknesses and potentials of the IFG and the gaps, the whole EL module and the SiPMs, the reference ion mobility and the electron diffusion.

5. What the simulation does

Simulated

  1. Geant4 in the gas of the cards, with the radius and length of each variant.
  2. Ion transport to the pad plane with the thermal spread of the variant and its ion velocity, on the 3 mm lattice with the frame of the variant.
  3. VH1 readout with 20 fF capacitors and linear reconstruction of the ion signal (VH1 readout).
  4. Hits and tracks from the reconstructed ion signal.

Emulated

  1. Fast trigger and event frame from the Geant4 deposits (MC fast trigger).
  2. Event energy: sum of the Geant4 deposits inside the box given to the ion response (IItaca.frame_energy_keV), smeared with a Gaussian of 0.47 % FWHM at Qββ, scaling as √E (IItaca.emulated_energy_keV).

Order of the cuts

  1. Fast trigger: energy window, fiducial selection, gamma veto on deposits outside the frame.
  2. ROI cut on the emulated energy: 2452.03 to 2463.63 keV.
  3. Track selection on the ion lattice: components at 5.5 mm, detached deposits, segments rejoined at 8 mm (track selection).
  4. Endpoint-energy cut or classification network (classification network).

6. Cards

The detector set is config/iitaca/iitaca.toml: nine cards (gas, geometry, drift, voltages, ions, energy, el, trigger, tracks) with design values only. Values under a [documentation] table are not passed to the simulation (IItaca.simulation_inputs); changing one changes this page and no result. A variant is a card with overrides, dotted keys into the set. A run card names the set, optionally a variant, and may add its own overrides, applied after those of the variant: radius, length, pressure, fields, potentials, pitch, frame and energy resolution are all changed that way.

When the cards are resolved (IItaca.resolve_detector) the result is checked: lengths, fields, velocity and frame positive; ion velocity × frame equal to the length drifted in one frame of the ions card; the frame of the VH1 card equal to the frame of the detector; potentials rising from the pads to the IFG.

8. Sources

  • Design values: user design inputs of 2026-11-17, including the ion drift velocities.
  • Gas density: NEXT DocDB 1183 (xenon compressibility); isotopic fractions of NEXUS GXeEnriched.
  • Ion mobility: P. N. B. Neves et al., J. Chem. Phys. 133 (2010) 124316, as plotted in A. F. V. Cortez et al., JINST 12 (2017) P06012, Fig. 3.
  • Ion thermal spread: ITACA revisited, arXiv:2604.02443, Eq. (2.1); implementation in src/event_analysis/production.jl.
  • Electron diffusion in pure xenon: NEXT-DEMO++ paper, papers/xehe_paper.pdf, Table 1. EL yield: C. M. B. Monteiro et al., JINST 2 (2007) P05001, as used in Section 4.4 of that paper.
  • Potentials of the ion plane: manuscripts/itaca3/itaca3.tex (IFG at 8 kV, sensors at ground).
  • Energy resolution: full simulation of the Xe/He electron readout (comparison of bb0nu and single electrons). Qββ: M. Redshaw et al., PRL 98 (2007) 053003.

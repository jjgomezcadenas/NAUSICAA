Plots and event viewer

Display

  1. Read event totals for the energy distributions.
  2. Select an MC event and read its hits and graph results.
  3. Show energy heatmaps with backbone overlays in XY, XZ and YZ.
  4. Show the backbones in 3D.
  • Spatial limits enclose both tracks with 25% extra space. Axes use mm.
  • “Zoom to track” limits every view of both tabs to one cube around the main tracks: the union of the boxes of the largest graph component of each track, with a margin of 10 mm or 10% of its largest side, kept inside the frame. Gamma deposits may fall outside. The cube is saved per event by the Julia step; the heatmaps are not recomputed. A second press returns to the full frame, which is the default; the state is kept when the event changes.
  • Disable zoom and pan. Allow 3D rotation.
  • Use a fixed-size display panel with itrack and etrack tabs.
  • Changing the event updates the views and shows both drift distances.

The current viewer reads selected events from the reconstructed hit file. Projections show summed hit energies with backbone overlays; 3D shows backbones only. Python regenerates the page from saved presentation data.

Statistics of a run kept as batches

  1. prepare_batch_statistics.jl results/readout/<run_label> reads every batches/b0000N/<output_subdir>/hits.h5 and the selection counts of each batch.
  2. For each accepted event and each track it voxelises the hits above the display cut, builds the graph and takes the component with most voxels as the main track.
  3. It saves one row per event in <output_subdir>/statistics/events.h5: energies, vertex, drift lengths, frame shift, and for each track the hits, components, main and second component energies, track length, extensions and endpoint energies.
  4. It fits the ion and electron energy peaks, for all events and in six drift intervals, and adds the selection counts per batch and in total.
  5. present_batch_statistics.py results/readout/<run_label> [classification.toml] writes the plots and statistics/index.html.
QuantityDefinition
Track lengthSum of the distances between consecutive voxels of the tree path joining the two endpoints of the main track.
Extension in X, Y, ZRange of the voxel centres of the main track plus one voxel width. Extension in R is the larger of X and Y.
E₁, E₂Energies in spheres around the two endpoints, E₁ ≥ E₂.

Two samples

  1. prepare_species_comparison.jl SIGNAL_events.h5 BACKGROUND_events.h5 OUTPUT_DIR applies the cut E₂ > threshold to both samples, for each track, with all accepted events and with the events of one component.
  2. It saves the signal efficiency and the background acceptance against the threshold, the area under that curve, and the background acceptance with its binomial error at signal efficiencies of 90, 80, 70, 60 and 50%.
  3. present_species_comparison.py OUTPUT_DIR overlays the distributions of the two samples, normalised to unit area, and draws the curves.

Monte Carlo classification of detached components

classify_run_batches.jl OUTPUT.toml DESCRIPTION [--ion-links=7,8] RUN_CARD ... compares the detached components of every batch with the Monte Carlo gamma deposits and writes the counts of one-component, floating-gamma and broken-track events. It needs the Monte Carlo files of the run. Commands: make itaca-statistics RUN_DIR=... and make itaca-comparison SIGNAL=... BACKGROUND=... OUT=....

eITACA IE_L1500_D400 against iItaca 2 mm: results

The same analysis on the electron track of eITACA (Xe with 15.8 % He, 16 bar, 1.5 m drift at 400 V/cm, SiPMs of 4 mm pitch) and on the ion track of iItaca II_L1000_D500_P2 (pure Xe, 16 bar, 1.0 m drift, 2 mm pads). The event energy is the energy in the simulated box smeared with 0.472 % FWHM at Qββ in both, so that the comparison isolates the topology.

The eITACA track settings are those of config/eitaca/tracks.toml, set by the rules of the iItaca cards in cells of the etrack lattice. The components radius is just above the cell diagonal, 6.17 mm; on these flat cells it also joins cells two buckets apart in z (4.9 mm). The minimum segment counts the hits of 18 mm of track, measured on the selected bb0nu tracks in the ROI: diffusion spreads the track over several SiPMs, so a segment needs more hits than on the pad lattice. The join radius is 8 mm × 4 mm / 3 mm. Noise of one hit (one SiPM over 20 buckets: ASIC 0.5 PE per bucket, median 0.175 dark pulses per bucket, 2.263 eV per PE): median 6.7 eV, at most 6.8 eV; the noise sets no cut above the saved one. The ROI is one FWHM around Qββ (2452.0–2463.6 keV). The network threshold gives 80 % of the bb0nu test events of each training.

eITACA gamma backgrounds: the network acceptance is measured on the selected ²¹⁴Bi and ²⁰⁸Tl events with box energy in 2420–2470 keV, pooled (²¹⁴Bi alone 2549 of 67,250, 3.79 ± 0.07 %; ²⁰⁸Tl alone 63 of 1,234, 5.11 ± 0.63 %), and applied to the selected events of each isotope. The ²⁰⁸Tl files hold no event above 2470 keV of deposited energy; those can reach the ROI only through the smearing, more than 1.3 σ, and are missing.

Cut flow, ROI last

The network fraction is measured on the selected events and applied to all selected events: over this energy range the topology does not depend on the energy. For bb0nu and single electrons it is that of the test sample of the training. The ROI fraction is the fraction of the selected events whose energy is in the ROI.

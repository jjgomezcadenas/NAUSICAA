\section*{Configuration}
Each of the two 1 mm Geant4 samples uses one shared event placement and one Fano-sampled primary-pair realisation. Its ions are collected on three pad grids. The electron branch uses the same realization and is propagated once per sample, producing separate pure-Xe and Xe--15\% He incident-photon histories. The electron settings are identical for all ion-grid choices.
